# Meeting Summary: Company Name Debate — Worldwide Brand from Hispanic Markets

**Date:** July 25, 2026  
**Agents Present:** CEO, CPO, CGO  
**Topic:** Choose a company name for a web-builder SaaS that starts in Hispanic markets (Spain + Colombia) but is designed as a long-term worldwide brand.

**Amendment (same day, founder):** Founder owns **`clubtal.com`** — zero internet footprint (no competing products, companies, or social handles). All shortlisted dictionary names (`Listo`, `Presto`, `Sello`, `Nítido`, `Nimbo`) have existing commercial references and unavailable or expensive domains. **Decision revised: Clubtal is the company name.**

---

## Each Agent's Key Points (Condensed)

### CEO
- Name must be **vertical-agnostic** — platform already pivoted once (beauty → repair shops).
- Seven gates every candidate must pass: voice-note, ceceo (no z/ce/ci), Romance-generic, vertical-lock, LatAm mega-brand collision, ownership (`.com`/`.co`), Instagram handle.
- Initial shortlist: **Listo** (lead), Nimbo, Sello, Nítido, Faro — with Listo, Presto, and Sello all failing ownership (`.com` aftermarket or taken).
- Killed: `Vitrina` (generic in French), `Nubo` (crowded cloud namespace), `TuWeb`/`MiWeb` constructions (scam pattern in cold DMs).
- Rebrand at 200 clients is expensive — optimize for never renaming.

### CPO
- **Listo** best encodes product promise ("ready today") but needs visual identity to carry premium weight.
- Introduced **Presto** as strong #2 — global legibility, "hey presto" magic-reveal story.
- Demoted **Nimbo** — meaningless at zero marketing budget is expensive, not safe.
- Added 8th gate: read the name cold in a WhatsApp DM to a repair shop owner.
- Flagged stale pricing docs (still reference €19/mo beauty vertical).

### CGO
- **Listo scores 9.5/10** for cold WhatsApp — name IS the value proposition in a 3-line message.
- Presto only as trademark-conflict fallback, not a genuine outreach challenger.
- Three-role convergence on Listo — but all three assumed `.com` availability was solvable.

### Founder Amendment — Clubtal
- **`clubtal.com` already owned.** No search results, social handles, or commercial products associated with the term.
- Coined brand (like Canva, Spotify) — empty vessel, fully ownable, globally legible.
- Passes all 7+1 gates: voice-note ("Club-tal"), ceceo, vertical-agnostic, no Romance-generic conflict, clean trademark namespace, domain owned, social handles likely available.
- "Club" signals belonging/community; "-tal" suffix reads as substance (digital/portal) in English and warmth ("¿qué tal?") in Spanish.
- Weakness vs Listo: no instant Spanish emotional hook — descriptor line must carry the pitch in year 1.
